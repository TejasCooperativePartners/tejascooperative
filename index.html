The Basics

What a cooperative is, in plain terms

A cooperative purchasing organization pools the buying power of many public agencies into one program. It runs one competitive proposal process per category and awards contracts to selected vendors. Every member agency can then buy from those vendors under the cooperative contract, which satisfies the member's own bidding rules.

The Cooperative

The gatekeeper. It posts proposal invitations by category, takes applications, evaluates them, and awards contracts. Texas vendors meet names such as BuyBoard (formally The Local Government Purchasing Cooperative, administered by the Texas Association of School Boards) and TIPS (The Interlocal Purchasing System), alongside national cooperatives with members in every state. The next section lists the cooperatives we work with.

The Members

The buyers. School districts, cities, counties, community colleges, and state agencies join the cooperative so they can purchase from awarded vendors quickly and within procurement law.

The Vendors

The sellers. That is you. An award in your category lets every member buy your products and services under one contract, at or below your awarded pricing, for the full contract term and its renewals.

Why Vendors Call Us

Good companies get rejected every cycle

The cooperative process rewards complete, correct, on-time applications. Most vendors fail on process, not on the quality of what they sell.

Rejection

Applications get turned down for incomplete submissions, missed requirements, and weak supporting documents. A rejection costs the vendor the whole cycle.

No time to finish

The requirements are long and detailed. Owners and sales staff at busy companies start the paperwork, get pulled back to daily work, and the window closes.

Recommendation letters

Proposals call for recommendation letters from cooperative member entities. A vendor with no member relationships has no practical way to gather strong letters on a deadline. Our firm does, through relationships built over years.

Categories open on the cooperative's schedule. Miss your category window and the next opening can be years away.

How It Works

From first call to award letter

  1. Fit review

    We review your products, services, and territory, check your current cooperative status, and confirm a live or upcoming invitation in your category.

  2. Agreement and profile

    You sign the consulting agreement and complete your company profile once in our platform. That profile is reused on every proposal that follows.

  3. Invitation watch

    Our platform watches for your category opening and flags addenda and deadline changes the day they post.

  4. Package build

    We build the requirements checklist, close every gap, gather recommendation letters, and prepare your pricing and catalog material.

  5. Submission and evaluation

    We coordinate your submission before the deadline and work with you through the cooperative's questions during evaluation.

  6. Award and renewals

    On the award notice, your contract term starts. We record your awarded items and renewal dates, and line up your next category while your first contract sells.

Questions

Asked by nearly every vendor

Are you part of BuyBoard or TIPS?

No. Tejas Cooperative Partners is an independent consulting firm. We help vendors apply to cooperatives including BuyBoard, TIPS, and the other cooperatives listed on this page. We hold no official role with any cooperative or its administrator, and award decisions rest entirely with each cooperative.

We already tried and got rejected. Can you still help?

Yes, and rejected applicants are a large share of the vendors we talk to. We review what your earlier submission contained, identify the gaps, and rebuild the package for the next opening in your category, with recommendation letters gathered through our member relationships.

How long does it take to get on the board?

Timing follows the cooperative's category calendar. Work starts when an invitation in your category is live or approaching, and the evaluation period runs on the cooperative's schedule. We tell you the expected window for your category at the fit review, before you sign.

What does the award actually let us sell?

The items and services marked as awarded to you in the cooperative's tabulation for your invitation, at or below your awarded pricing, to any member agency in the cooperative, for the contract term and its renewals.

Do you guarantee an award or a level of sales?

No. We guarantee a complete, correct, on-time application, built and managed by people who have done it. The cooperative decides awards, and member agencies decide what to buy and from whom.

Why a flat fee instead of a percentage of sales?

A flat fee keeps the engagement simple and keeps our work focused on your award. Your sales under the contract belong to you, in full, at any volume.
